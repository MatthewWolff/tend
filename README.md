# Tend

**Tend your friendships.** A personal relationship manager for iOS, built for people who care about staying connected but struggle to keep track.

## The Problem

Life gets busy. You think about a friend, mean to reach out, and then three months go by. For people with ADHD especially, maintaining friendships isn't about caring less — it's about the gap between intention and action.

## How Tend Helps

Tend gives you a simple system: set how often you want to see each friend, and the app takes care of the rest.

### Relationship Circles

Friends are organized into four circles based on how often you connect:

- **Inner Circle** — Your closest people. Weekly or more.
- **Close Friends** — Regular catch-ups. Every couple of weeks.
- **Regulars** — Monthly-ish check-ins.
- **Casual** — A few times a year.

Circles adjust automatically as your patterns change, or you can set them yourself.

### Stay on Track

After you log a meetup — dinner, a call, coffee, whatever — Tend calculates when you're next due to connect. You'll see at a glance who's overdue, who's coming up soon, and who you're on track with.

Gentle reminders nudge you when a friend is overdue. No spam — just the people who matter.

### Log Connections in Seconds

Had coffee with a friend? Long-press their name and log it in two taps. Or open the full form to add notes, a location, and more detail. Tend learns your patterns over time.

### Insights

- See where friends actually fall based on your real connection pace vs. where you want them
- Track who reaches out more — you or them
- Spot friends you're losing touch with before it's too late

## Privacy First

- All data stays on your device by default
- Optional encrypted cloud backup
- No ads, no analytics, no tracking
- You can delete everything at any time

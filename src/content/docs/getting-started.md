# Getting Started

Welcome to Tend! This guide walks you through the basics: adding friends, setting frequencies, and logging your first connection.

## 1. Sign in

Download Tend from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tend-outreach/id6760388237) and sign in with your Google or Apple account. Your account is used for optional cloud backup — all data is stored locally on your device by default.

If you want early access to upcoming features before they ship publicly, you can also join the [TestFlight beta](https://testflight.apple.com/join/HvuJgSBe).

## 2. Add your first friend

Tap the **+** button on the Home screen, or go to the **Friends** tab and tap **Add Friend**.

You can enter a friend manually or import from your iOS Contacts. Imported contacts automatically link so you can sync updated info later.

### What to fill in

- **Name** — required
- **Nickname** — optional, shown instead of the full name
- **Frequency** — how often you want to connect (e.g., every 2 weeks)
- **Birthday, phone, email, address** — optional, useful for reminders and transit times

## 3. Set a frequency

The frequency determines how often Tend reminds you to reach out. Each friend can have their own frequency:

| Frequency | Good for |
|---|---|
| Every few days | Your closest friends, daily check-ins |
| Every 1–2 weeks | Close friends you see regularly |
| Every 3–5 weeks | Good friends you catch up with monthly |
| Every 2+ months | Casual acquaintances, seasonal catch-ups |

You can change the frequency anytime from the friend's profile.

## 4. Log a connection

When you interact with a friend, log it! Long-press a friend in the **Next Due** list for quick options:

- **Call, Text, Hangout, Video** — logs a connection instantly
- **Skip this time** — advances the due date without logging

Or tap the **+** button to log a detailed connection with notes, location, and time.

## 5. Check your dashboard

The **Home** tab shows:

- **Quick Stats** — connections this week, overdue count
- **Next Due** — friends sorted by urgency
- **Upcoming** — scheduled future connections
- **Recent** — your latest interactions

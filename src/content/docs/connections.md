# Connections

Connections are the core of Tend — they represent any interaction with a friend. A phone call, a dinner, a text thread, a video chat — all are connections.

## Logging a connection

### Quick log (long-press)

The fastest way: long-press a friend in the **Next Due** list and pick a type (Call, Text, Hangout, Video). The connection is logged instantly at the current time.

### Full form

Tap the **+** button for the detailed form:

- **Friends** — select one or more friends (group events)
- **Date & time** — when it happened (or will happen)
- **Type** — call, text, dinner, coffee, or any custom type
- **Level** — how meaningful was it? (High, Medium, Low)
- **Who initiated** — you, them, or mutual
- **Notes** — anything you want to remember
- **Location** — where you met (enables transit time estimates)

## Connection types

You can use any connection type — Tend learns from what you log and suggests types based on the day of the week. Common types:

- Call, Text, Video, Hangout
- Coffee, Lunch, Dinner
- Walk, Workout, Game night

## Group events

Select multiple friends when logging a connection to create a group event. Tend creates:

- **One event** shared by all participants
- **One connection per friend** linked to that event
- **One calendar entry** with all names (e.g., "Dinner with Sarah, Mike, and Alex")

Removing a friend from a group event updates the calendar title automatically.

## Calendar sync

When calendar sync is enabled in Settings, future timed connections automatically appear in your iOS Calendar.

- Events include the friend's name, time, and location
- Configurable reminder offset (15min, 30min, 1hr, 2hr)
- Tap **Sync Now** in Settings to resync all upcoming events

## Future connections

You can log connections in the future (planned meetups). These:

- Show in the **Upcoming** tab on the Home screen
- Sync to your iOS Calendar if enabled
- Don't count toward the friend's "last connection" until the date passes

## Editing and deleting

- **Edit**: tap a connection to open the detail sheet, then tap **Edit Connection**
- **Delete**: scroll to the bottom of the detail sheet and tap **Delete Connection**
- Deleting a connection recalculates the friend's next due date from remaining connections
